### Circle to Search for Windows (10/11)

Initial open-source release of **CircleSearch**!

#### Highlights:
- **Hold Win Key Trigger:** Summon overlay anywhere without opening the Start Menu (also supports `Alt + Space`).
- **Interactive & Resizable Selection:** Draw and fine-tune your crop area with 8 resize handles and full drag-and-drop movement.
- **Embedded Google Lens Browser:** Real-time visual search, copyable text, and instant translations directly in a side panel.
- **Multi-Monitor & Mixed DPI Ready:** True PerMonitorV2 support. Accurately captures high-resolution displays (2K, 4K, 2880x1800).
- **12 Built-in Languages:** English, Kazakh, Russian, Spanish, German, French, Portuguese, Turkish, Ukrainian, Chinese, Japanese, and Korean.
- **100% Free & Open-Source:** No API keys or subscriptions required.

#### Downloads:
- **Windows Installer (Recommended):** `CircleSearch-v1.0.0-win-x64.msi`
- **Portable ZIP:** `CircleSearch-v1.0.0-win-x64-portable.zip`
